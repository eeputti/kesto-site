privacy policy

how kesto handles your data.

who we are

kesto ("the kesto system") is a training app published by eelis puro, finland. we are the controller of the personal data described here. contact: kestosystem@gmail.com.

what we collect

  • an account id that the app creates automatically the first time you open it. you don't need an email or a password.
  • if you choose sign in with apple: your apple user id, and the name or email address apple shares if you allow it.
  • your onboarding and profile answers: goals, training days, experience, equipment, sleep schedule, and optionally your first name, height, weight and gender.
  • your training: planned and completed sessions, sets, weights, distances, effort, notes, reasons for skipped sessions and recovery check-ins.
  • apple health data, only if you allow it: workouts, steps, resting heart rate and heart rate variability. kesto only reads this data and never writes to apple health. it lets kesto adapt your plan and count workouts from polar, garmin and other watches that sync to apple health.
  • ask kesto messages, only if you turn on ai data sharing.
  • anonymous usage statistics (for example which screens and features are used), without your name, health data or any advertising identifier.

how we use it

  • to build and adapt your training plan,
  • to show your progress,
  • to save your data to your account so it is not lost,
  • to answer you in ask kesto, if you turned it on,
  • to fix bugs and improve kesto.

we never sell your data, never use it for advertising and never track you across other companies' apps or websites.

ask kesto (ai coach)

ask kesto is optional and asks for your permission before anything is sent. when you send a message, kesto sends it to openai (openai, l.l.c., usa) together with your training context: your plan and logged sessions, the goals and preferences from onboarding, and apple health workouts and recovery data if you connected apple health.

openai uses this only to write the reply. under openai's api terms it is not used to train their models; openai may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse. your conversation is also saved to your kesto account so the coach remembers it.

you can turn ai data sharing off at any time in you → coach → ai data sharing.

apple health

apple health data is read only with your permission and used only for kesto's training features. it is never used for advertising, never sold, and kesto does not store it in icloud. change access at any time in the health app → sharing → apps → kesto.

where your data is stored and who processes it

on your phone, and in our backend hosted by supabase in frankfurt, germany (eu).

  • supabase: database and hosting (eu)
  • openai: ask kesto replies (usa), only with your permission
  • telemetrydeck: anonymous usage statistics (eu)
  • apple: sign in with apple and payments

transfers to the usa are protected by the eu–us data privacy framework and/or the eu standard contractual clauses.

payments

subscriptions are handled by apple. we never see your card or payment details, only whether your subscription is active.

legal basis

we process your data to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract), with your explicit consent for health data and ai data sharing (article 9(2)(a) gdpr), and on our legitimate interest for security and anonymous statistics. you can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing that happened before.

how long we keep it

as long as you have a kesto account. deleting your account in you → account settings → delete account permanently removes your profile, training data and ask kesto history from our backend right away. data on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

your rights

you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, move it to another service, object to processing or withdraw consent by emailing kestosystem@gmail.com. you can also complain to the finnish data protection ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi) or your local data protection authority.

children

kesto is not meant for children under 13.

changes

if this policy changes, we update the date below and tell you in the app when the change matters.
