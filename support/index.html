support

help with kesto, the hybrid training app.

contact

email kestosystem@gmail.com — we usually answer within two working days.

cancel or change your subscription

subscriptions are managed by apple: settings → your name → subscriptions → kesto. deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

restore a purchase

open the paywall in kesto and tap "restore purchases" while signed in with the same apple id.

connect polar, garmin, suunto or coros

kesto reads workouts from apple health. turn on apple health sync in your watch's app (polar flow: settings → general settings → apple health; garmin connect: settings → connected apps → apple health), then allow kesto to read apple health.

turn off ai data sharing

you → coach → ai data sharing. ask kesto stops sending anything to openai.

delete your account and data

you → account settings → delete account. this permanently deletes your data from our backend. remember to cancel your subscription separately in your apple id settings.
